Tricky Piles: Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Tricky Piles is a puzzle game for Android. You can play as a guest. Where Google sign-in is available, you can optionally connect an account to back up your progress. This page explains what data the game handles, why, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

Daniel Inverno, the developer of Tricky Piles. Contact: Inverno3darte@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, best times, stars and settings are saved on your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them, but Android's own backup may have copied them (with the player id below) to your Google account and restore them when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. You manage that backup in your phone's settings (Google → Backup).

Optional Google account and cloud progress

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase process your Google identity, including the email address and profile information returned by Google. You never give the game a Google password. You may choose a separate nickname; your Google name and email are not published to other players.

The connected account stores your unlocked level, completed levels, stars, best times, best scores and tutorial checkpoints in Supabase so the same Google account can recover them on another installation. Settings, the current attempt and purchase ownership are not included. Offline changes stay on the device until a cloud save is confirmed.

Signing out keeps a private local checkpoint for that account on this installation. Signing out or uninstalling does not delete the account or its cloud copy. Reset progress while connected resets the cloud checkpoint across devices, but does not delete the account or nickname. Cloud progress and nicknames are private; connecting an account does not enroll you in a public leaderboard.

What is sent, and why

Data Why Your choice
A random player id. Created by the game the first time it goes online. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account while playing as a guest. After optional Google sign-in, new results use your connected account. The id links results together, so it is pseudonymous rather than anonymous. To rank your times and to avoid counting the same result twice. Ends when you uninstall the game or clear its data (unless Android restores it from a backup, see above).
Your best time per level (level number, time, game version), with the player id. To show how your time compares with other players ("Faster than 80% of players"). Other players never see your times. Zen mode times are never sent.
Level results: for each attempt, the level, whether it was the daily level, whether you cleared it, failed or left it, the mistakes by type, the time, the number of hands, whether you played in zen mode or used the continue, when the attempt ended, and the game and app versions. To find levels that are too hard or too easy and improve the game. Optional: turn off Share play stats in Settings. Results not yet sent are deleted on the phone.
Connection data: the IP address and device or browser type of each session, recorded by the sign-in service that issues the player id. Security and abuse prevention of the service. Comes with going online; nothing is sent when you play offline.
Crash reports: the error, device model, Android and app versions, and an install id added by the crash reporting tool. To find and fix crashes. Sent only when the game crashes or hits an error. The crash service is set not to store IP addresses.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos or messages.

Ads

When you lose a level, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to continue. Ads are shown by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect your device's advertising id, an approximate location derived from your IP address, device information, diagnostics, and how you interact with the ad, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice the first time you choose to watch an ad (never before you play). You can change it any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising id in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).

The Remove ads purchase makes the continue free without ads.

Purchases

The Remove ads purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the game only stores on your phone whether the purchase is owned.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase stores the player id, connection data, best times and level results, and optional account identity, nickname and cloud progress (servers in the EU, Ireland).
  • Sentry receives crash reports (servers in the EU, Germany).

Both process the data on our behalf under their data processing terms. Google shows the ads (AdMob) and handles purchases (Google Play) as an independent controller, and authenticates optional Google sign-in, under Google's privacy policy. We do not sell data. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Legal basis (EU and UK)

Rankings, level results, connection data and crash reports rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game; you can opt out of level results in Settings. Personalised ads rely on your consent, given in Google's consent form.

How long data is kept

Best times and level results are kept while the game is available, and are used only in totals for game tuning. Optional account identity, nickname and cloud progress remain until account deletion; progress reset replaces the checkpoint while retaining a reset marker to prevent an older device restoring it. Crash reports are deleted by Sentry after at most 90 days.

Your rights

To delete a connected game account, open Settings → Nickname → Delete account and confirm twice while online. This removes the account identity, nickname, cloud progress, and linked private times and level results. It also clears that account's local checkpoint on the device used for deletion. It leaves your Google account and Google Play purchases intact. Updating the game and signing in do not delete progress.

You can also request account deletion by email without reinstalling the game. Server deletion cannot erase copies on offline devices or Android-managed backups; clear local game data and remove unwanted backups separately.

You can ask us about the data we hold, or ask us to delete it, at Inverno3darte@gmail.com. Because we do not know who a guest player is, we may need you to describe when and which levels you played to find your data. For a connected account, identify the Google email used to sign in; we may verify ownership before processing a request. Uninstalling the game and removing it from your Android backup removes the player id from your phone, and new data can no longer be linked to the old id. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Tricky Piles is meant for players aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
