Tricky Piles: Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Tricky Piles is a puzzle game for Android. You play without an account: we never ask for your name, email or any other personal details. This page explains what data the game handles, why, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

Daniel Inverno, the developer of Tricky Piles. Contact: Inverno3darte@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, best times, stars and settings are saved on your phone only. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

What is sent, and why

Data Why Your choice
An anonymous player id. A random id created by the game the first time it goes online. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account. To rank your times and to avoid counting the same result twice. Ends when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
Your best time per level (level number, time, game version), with the anonymous id. To show how your time compares with other players ("Faster than 80% of players"). Other players never see your times. Zen mode times are never sent.
Level results: for each attempt, the level, whether you cleared it, failed or left it, the mistakes by type, the time, the number of hands, and whether you used the continue. To find levels that are too hard or too easy and improve the game. Optional: turn off Share play stats in Settings. Results not yet sent are deleted on the phone.
Crash reports: the error, device model, Android and app versions, and an install id added by the crash reporting tool. To find and fix crashes. Sent only when the game crashes or hits an error.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, messages or any personal identifiers.

Ads

When you lose a level, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to continue. Ads are shown by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect your device's advertising id, an approximate location derived from your IP address, device information, diagnostics, and how you interact with the ad, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice the first time you choose to watch an ad (never before you play). You can change it any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising id in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).

The Remove ads purchase makes the continue free without ads.

Purchases

The Remove ads purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the game only stores on your phone whether the purchase is owned.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase stores the anonymous id, best times and level results (servers in the EU, Ireland).
  • Sentry receives crash reports (servers in the EU, Germany).
  • Google (AdMob and Google Play) for ads and purchases.

These services process the data on our behalf under their own data protection terms. We do not sell data. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Legal basis (EU and UK)

Rankings, level results and crash reports rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game; you can opt out of level results in Settings. Personalised ads rely on your consent, given in Google's consent form.

How long data is kept

Best times and level results are kept while the game is available, and are used only in totals (no profiles of individual players). Crash reports are deleted by Sentry after at most 90 days.

Your rights

You can ask us about the data we hold, or ask us to delete it, at Inverno3darte@gmail.com. Because the data is anonymous, we may need you to describe when and which levels you played to find it. Uninstalling the game removes the anonymous id from your phone, and new data can no longer be linked to the old id. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Tricky Piles is meant for players aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
