Electrical enclosure

SolidWorks · 2016 · Final project of the 3D CAD module

  • Modeled a sheet-metal electrical enclosure (housing, door and mounting plate) in 3D from supplied 2D drawings.
  • Assembled it with supplied vendor components: EMKA hinges and lock, a Pfannenberg filter fan, 17 PG7 cable glands for cable entry, DIN fasteners, weld studs, and earthing studs with a ground wire.
  • From my own model, produced a 7-sheet drawing set: views, details, a section, tolerances, weld symbols, sheet-metal flat patterns with bend notes, and a 24-item bill of materials.

Electronics module · Final project

Arduino line-follower robot

Built in a team of two.

  • Two 9 V DC motors on an acrylic chassis.
  • TCRT5000 infrared sensors.
  • TIP122 NPN transistors driving the motors.
  • Arduino code for the sensor and motor logic.
  • A sensor bracket bent from aluminum sheet, center-punched and drilled.
  • A power-supply PCB designed in KiCad, etched and soldered.
  • Bench-tested on a lab supply, and completed.

In progress

Autodesk Inventor

HPU-01 In progress

A hydraulic power unit on a skid, modeled in Autodesk Inventor from a design spec, with guided design input on component selection and details. Planned: Frame Generator base, motor-pump group, CETOP 3 valve block, steel piping (Tube & Pipe) and cable routing (Cable & Harness), with a GA drawing, a piping isometric and a frame cut list.

Done so far: a 40 L sheet-metal tank (3 mm steel, 0.5 mm weld gaps at the corners) with its flat pattern; a 6 mm bolted lid; weld-in half sockets (ISO 4145) for the suction port and the drain; and a vendor STEP return filter (MP Filtri MPTX), all constrained in one assembly with no interferences.
